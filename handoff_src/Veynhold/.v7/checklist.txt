VEYNHOLD — Sekiro-inspired combat prototype, v2 "feel pass" (single self-contained file; Three.js r128 embedded, no network)

ARCHITECTURE (kept): pure deterministic combat core (CONFIG / ATTACKS / PROFILES, seeded RNG, fixed 60 Hz ticks)
+ presentation layer (procedural rig + POSES, camera, FX, audio, HUD). The core never touches THREE or the DOM.

V2 CHANGES
[x] Parry reliability: window opens on guard PRESS, anchored to the visual strike frame, 70 ms late grace;
    no spam shrink for rhythmic deflects (only 4+ presses/s, reset by any clean deflect); no swallowed presses;
    0.18 s guard buffer that cancels out of attack recovery, dodge and the end of hit-stun; 240° deflect arc +
    auto-turn to the nearest threat; feedback only on accepted presses; per-fighter hit-stop that never drops input.
[x] Smoothness: accel/decel locomotion, speed-dependent turning, start lean + stop skid, everything visual
    interpolated (position, facing, jump arc, attack/dodge timers), critically damped camera springs, smooth-noise shake,
    FOV kicks, camera hold on hit-stop.
[x] Animation: 5-key attack families (anticipation, windup, strike, overshoot, follow-through + damped settle),
    root motion as a bell curve on the strike, per-joint springs, distance-driven gait (no foot slide), directional
    hit reactions, distinct block recoil vs deflect snap, breathing / sway, dense sub-frame sword trails.
[x] Perilous counters: thrust -> dodge into it (Counterstep posture break), sweep -> jump (+ mid-air trample),
    grab -> dodge (whiff opening + posture recovery). AI defends by the same rules with human-like timing error.
[x] Patterns: per-swing timing jitter + variable held windups, irregular-beat strings (1-7 hits), mid-string branches
    (on deflect / dodge / back-off), deflect+jump+dodge mix-ups, footwork (side-steps, gap-closers, feint steps),
    tempo modes (pressure / measured / patient), phase II tempo + new strings. Watchdog + token system kept.
[x] Live tuning panel (` then T, or Pause > Tune feel), accessibility large deflect window.

  deflects with no lock-on facing 90° and 150° away, buffered presses from attack recovery / dodge / hit-stun,
  ±50 ms human timing = 100% deflects, mashing never beats one good press, spam window resets on deflect,
  hit-stop input buffering, jitter bounds, branching strings, 6-hit mix-up, Counterstep, trample, grab whiff,

- Motion smoothness: on-screen position steps vary 0.0% frame to frame during steady movement at 144/60/30 fps.

  4 of 5; button-mashing wins 32% (mostly the Duelist), holding guard loses every fight.

v5 — ANIMATION DEPTH, PLAYER KIT, KING PHASE II IDENTITY
Core (deterministic, tested):
- Light chain is 4 hits (4th = spinning finisher, wide arc, big knockback). Light…Heavy = rising finisher. Heavy within 0.6 s of a deflect = riposte. Light out of a dodge = dash cut; while sprinting = running thrust; in the air = plunge (once per jump).
- King Phase II: Crimson Arc travelling wave (deflect / dodge through / jump; deflecting it hurts his posture), blocks of his blade chip health (18% of damage), new wave strings.
- Enemy attackStart events flag the last swing of a string.
Presentation:
- 14 new attack families + reaction, execution, victim and awakening poses; stance-relative families per character (stance x style x light/heavy/perilous follow-through = 252 families).
- Distinct combat stances (duelist fencer, warden low and wide, brother low crouch, king regal, king II predatory "crimson stance").
- Living stance: posture strain, low health and AI tempo mode reshape the idle.
- Every directly driven state blends in from the previous pose; chained swings blend longer; swing-to-swing variation.
- Hit escalation (alternating flinches, stumble at 3+ hits / heavy).
- Per-enemy deathblows (heart pierce, crown splitter, turning cut, kick-thrust, Kingsfall) and per-enemy victim reactions and death falls.
- King awakening sequence (kneel, rise, blade drag, roar) + crimson aura.
- Readability: white glint before delayed strikes (feints never glint), low drum on the last swing of a string, eye flash when an enemy branches, chain pips on HUD, finisher FX.
Tests: core 61/61, flow/input 42/42, smoke clean, models 27/27, v5 11/11; real headless Chrome renders all of it with no page errors.
