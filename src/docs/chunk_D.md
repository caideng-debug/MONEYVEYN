# Chunk D: The Headsman (Odran Vale)

All of it lives in `src/world_boss.js` (world layer only, loaded by `build.py` after the world scripts). `src/core.js` is untouched (md5 74a5299e9a45d86aaddc90e2f1831821) and every sim tick / state duration is the core's own: the chunk only changes poses, the attacker's visual root, blade attachment rotation, presentation time scale (`Cine.timeScale`), cameras and DOM overlays.

## What it adds
1. **Build-up on the Headsman's Stair**: torches thin out as you climb (two lit at the foot, one on the landing, dead ones at the stair head), a fresh notice from Odran and the Warden's rule cut into the stone (both readable lore), WebAudio-synth bell / crows / a heartbeat that quickens with height, one remembered line (Tamsin's, or Ilsabet's if the crane was freed), and a faint visible fog threshold between two chain posts before the gate seals.
2. **First-encounter cutscene** (~44 s, 8 camera shots, 10 subtitled lines, letterboxed). Plays once per save, skippable with Esc / E (pad Y), respects `Settings.intros`. The sim is held through `G.Intro.active`, so nothing can be hit; input is cleared and both fighters are re-placed on exit. Odran's lines keep to the story bible: craftsman's pride, avoids the stones, never says the King knew (that is his dying confession).
3. **Boss fixes**: field foes are disengaged and sent home when the Block seals and cannot perceive / lock / strike through the sealed fog gate (`seg.sight`); the finisher camera tests `Region.camClear` so it never sits inside the ring wall; "Kneel by the headsman" is offered where he actually fell.
4. **Sekiro-style deathblows** (player in world mode): lunge in, the blade aimed through the victim's spine (30 % up hips->head for a standing stab, 84 % = down through the neck when the victim is on its knees), hit-stop, ink / blood spray per `Settings.hitStyle`, a ripping flick, slump to the knees and burn to ash. The boss's final blow gets a three-shot camera cut (entry profile, behind the victim on the exit wound, low wide on the slump); field foes get a ~0.8 s 3/4 cut (none on Low).
5. **Punctuation** (this pass): a white-to-red full-screen flash on the stab frame (scaled by `VISUAL.flashFx`), a short camera shake on the rip-out (offset applied after every camera blend, so it also shakes the finale camera), and a red brush-style stamp in the game's UI font (Georgia italic, skewed, with a ragged brush stroke under it): **SENTENCED** for Odran's final blow (his own word turned back on him), **DEATHBLOW** for every other deathblow. The stamp replaces the generic `#flash` DEATHBLOW banner in world mode. All of it is DOM opacity / transform plus a camera offset: no new meshes, textures or draw calls, no per-frame allocations, same cost on every tier.
