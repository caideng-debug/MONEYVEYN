HOW TO FIGHT

Deflect is everything. Press F (or right mouse) as the enemy blade lands. Pressing guard opens a deflect window of about 0.2 s, and you get a few frames of grace even if you're a hair late. A perfect deflect rings bright, costs no health, restores a little posture and cracks the enemy's.

Keep holding and you block: safe, but it fills your posture. Tapping in rhythm with each swing is the intended style. Frantic mashing (4+ presses a second) shrinks your window until your next clean deflect.

Posture (the gold bar) breaks when full. Break the enemy's and a red dot appears: press E for a deathblow.

(the red rune) means a perilous attack. Thrust: deflect it, or dodge into it for a Counterstep posture break. Sweep: jump C, then attack in mid-air to trample them. Grab: dodge it and they're left wide open.

A white glint on a blade means a delayed strike is releasing now; feints never glint. A low drum marks the last swing of a string. In his second phase the King's Crimson Arc travels the ground: deflect, dodge through, or jump it — and blocking his blade bleeds you.
